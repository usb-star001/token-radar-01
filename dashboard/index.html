    How implied value is computed

    Every row is scored by one function of the snapshot figures. No protocol name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked. The detail view recomputes each row from the same figures and checks it against the number board.json carries.

    implied = round( tvl_usd × category_multiple × (1 + clamp(change_7d, −40, +80) / 100 × 0.7) × (0.65 + 0.35 × min(age_days, 1095) / 1095) × (1 + 0.04 × min(chains − 1, 3)) )

    TVL and seven-day growth come from the snapshot. Age is the time since the protocol was first listed, the best record of how long it has run with no token; an unlisted age uses 365 days. Chain count rewards protocols live on more chains, capped at three extra chains.

    Farm plans: eight steps per protocol, derived from its category, chains, TVL and age. Capital is $250 at $5B TVL and up, $150 at $1B, $100 at $100M, otherwise $50. Hold is 60 days under 180 days old, 45 days under a year, otherwise 30 days. Gas is about $5–$15 on Ethereum, $0.10–$1 on L2s, about $0.01 on Solana, $1–$3 elsewhere.

    Disclaimer: this board says a protocol records no token in the snapshot, never that it has none — a protocol may hold a token its DefiLlama record does not carry. Implied value is the radar-2 model's own estimate from TVL, growth, age and chain count; it is not a price, not a valuation, and not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. Farming costs real money and gas, and a token, if one ever comes, may be worth nothing.
